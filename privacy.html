Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information Budd collects, why, and your rights over it. Budd is a personal cannabis logbook and community app. By using Budd you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Budd is operated by [YOUR LEGAL NAME], based in [COUNTRY]. For any privacy questions, contact us at support.buddapp@gmail.com. For users in the EU/EEA and UK, we act as the “data controller” for your personal data.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and never stored by us in readable form).
  • Profile: display name, bio, and avatar you choose to add.
  • Content you create: strains, experiences, ratings, notes, photos, coffeeshops, spots, comments, likes, and follows.
  • Logbook data you sync across your devices.
  • Basic technical and diagnostic data needed to run the service.

How we use your information

To provide and sync your logbook, operate community features (profiles, sharing, likes, comments, following), keep the service secure, and respond to your requests. We do not sell your personal data.

Legal bases (GDPR)

Where EU/UK law applies, we process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app), on the basis of your consent (for example, content you choose to share publicly), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the service safe and functional. You can withdraw consent at any time by unsharing content or deleting your account.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage) to run the app's accounts, database, and photo storage. Your data is processed on Google's infrastructure and may be stored on servers outside your country, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards. See Google's privacy policy for details.

What's public vs. private

Your private logbook is visible only to you (synced to your account). Content you explicitly share — shared strains, experiences, coffeeshops, spots, your profile, comments — is visible to other signed-in users. Spots require an extra confirmation before they become public.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete individual items at any time, or delete your entire account from Settings, which removes your profile, shared content, photos, likes, comments, follows, and login. Some backups or logs may persist for a limited period before being overwritten.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the app, or contact us at support.buddapp@gmail.com. EU/UK users may also complain to their local data protection authority.

Age restriction

Budd is intended only for adults of legal age for cannabis use in their jurisdiction, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security

We rely on industry-standard measures provided by our infrastructure partners to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “last updated” date above and, where appropriate, in the app.

Contact
